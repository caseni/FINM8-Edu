# M8 EDU English Content Coverage

Turkish remains the canonical launch language. English lesson content is enabled
module by module only when the full learning object is translated:

- lesson title and objective
- all lesson blocks, including Pro copy when present
- visual alt text and captions
- practical task prompt and choices
- quiz prompts, options, and explanations
- takeaway and risk disclaimer

`getEnglishCoverageReport()` reports complete and incomplete lessons so the UI
or editorial review can avoid presenting partially translated curriculum as
finished content.

Current executable coverage is 14 of 24 lessons:

- all six Market Foundations lessons;
- all six Chart Literacy lessons;
- the Risk and Uncertainty and Volatility lessons.

The remaining four Risk Management lessons and all six Behavior/Evidence
lessons stay in draft coverage until every required English field is completed
and reviewed.
