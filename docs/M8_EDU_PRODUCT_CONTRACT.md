# M8 EDU Product Contract

## Purpose

M8 EDU is FINM8's free learning layer. It improves financial and market literacy through short, evidence-based lessons without turning education into investment advice.

M8 EDU is not a separate course marketplace. It connects structured learning paths with explanations that originate from FINM8 analyses.

## Primary user

The first release serves beginner and intermediate users who already look at markets but need help understanding structure, evidence, and risk.

The experience must also remain usable for a person with no prior financial knowledge.

## Product principles

1. Risk before prediction.
2. One lesson teaches one concept.
3. A lesson takes approximately three to six minutes.
4. Simple language is the default; professional terminology is progressive.
5. Real examples must use timestamped and versioned FINM8 evidence.
6. Education must never become a buy or sell instruction.
7. Core literacy, quizzes, progress, and badges remain free.
8. AI explains approved content; it does not invent the canonical curriculum.
9. Turkish is the canonical launch language. English follows content validation.
10. Completion is less important than demonstrated understanding.

## Learning surfaces

### Learning paths

Users can follow structured paths for market literacy, chart reading, risk management, technical structures, fundamentals, and trading psychology.

### Learn from analysis

FINM8 analysis results can link to a relevant micro lesson. A BOS, CHoCH, liquidity, support/resistance, volatility, or risk observation should be explainable without leaving the analysis context.

### Personal review

Quiz attempts and practical tasks update a skill profile. Weak concepts return through short review sessions instead of forcing the user to repeat an entire course.

## Micro lesson contract

Every published lesson contains:

1. A question, situation, or chart observation.
2. A concise explanation of one concept.
3. At least one visual or chart-based example when relevant.
4. A common misconception or failure mode.
5. A short practical task.
6. Two to four assessment questions.
7. A one-sentence takeaway.
8. Source, review date, content version, and risk disclaimer metadata.

## Progress and badges

Progress is measured through concept mastery, not screen views. A badge requires lesson completion, quiz performance, and practical evidence where applicable.

Badges are learning achievements and must not be represented as official certificates or professional qualifications.

## Free and paid boundary

### Free

- Structured micro lessons
- Core quizzes and practical tasks
- Basic progress and skill map
- Learning badges
- Basic contextual explanations from FINM8 analyses

### Pro or Advisor

- Personalized learning plans
- Deeper explanations based on user history
- Advanced chart exercises
- Automated review schedules
- History and outcome-based scenarios
- Extended AI tutoring grounded in approved content

Paid features can improve personalization and depth but must not block essential financial literacy.

## Safety and content governance

- Canonical lessons require owner review before publication.
- Time-sensitive facts require a source and review date.
- Generated explanations must stay within approved lesson and evidence boundaries.
- Content must distinguish observation, interpretation, uncertainty, and action.
- No lesson may promise profit, imply certainty, or direct a user to trade.

## First release scope

The first release will not rebuild all nine legacy courses or all seven translations.

It will validate the system with:

- One level check
- One Turkish learning path
- 20 to 25 micro lessons
- A reusable lesson player
- Mini quizzes
- Progress and skill tracking
- Three to five meaningful badges
- At least one contextual link from a FINM8 analysis concept

## Initial success signals

- Lesson completion rate
- Quiz accuracy and improvement after review
- Practical task accuracy
- Return rate for scheduled reviews
- Use of contextual learning from analysis screens
- Reduction in repeatedly misunderstood concepts
- Content safety incidents

## Non-goals for the first release

- Official certificates
- Social leaderboards
- Trade-frequency rewards
- Seven-language launch
- AI-generated lessons published without review
- Full derivatives or algorithmic-trading curricula
