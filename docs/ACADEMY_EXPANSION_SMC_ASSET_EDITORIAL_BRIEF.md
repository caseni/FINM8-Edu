# FINM8 EDU — Academy Expansion Editorial Briefs: SMC/ICT + Asset Types

Standalone educational assets only. Never create a FINM8 UI mockup, dashboard screenshot, IDE/code scene, decorative finance wallpaper, or visual that presents a methodology label as objective institutional intent.

## SMC / ICT expansion hooks

### `order-block-smc-baglaminda-nasil-tanimlanir`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/smc-ict-expansion/smc-order-block-hook.*`
Teach: compare a candidate area tied to displacement/structural consequence with context-free “last opposite candle” selections. Criteria must precede outcome.

### `mitigation-ne-anlatir`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/smc-ict-expansion/smc-mitigation-hook.*`
Teach: first revisit, partial interaction, and invalidation of a previously defined area as separate observable scenarios. A touch is not a guaranteed reversal.

### `dealing-range-ve-equilibrium`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/smc-ict-expansion/smc-dealing-range-hook.*`
Teach: declared swing low/high defines a working range and approximate 50% equilibrium; show how a different valid range changes relative location.

### `premium-discount-ne-anlatir`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/smc-ict-expansion/smc-premium-discount-hook.*`
Teach: premium/discount are relative positions inside a declared dealing range, not statements that an asset is fundamentally expensive or cheap.

### `mss-choch-ile-ayni-mi`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/smc-ict-expansion/smc-mss-choch-hook.*`
Teach: one observable structural shift with two methodology definitions: one may label CHoCH, another MSS only after extra displacement/confirmation. Avoid declaring one universal label.

### `smc-confluence-nasil-kullanilmali`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/smc-ict-expansion/smc-confluence-hook.*`
Teach: sweep, displacement, FVG, order block, and MSS derived from the same move are correlated evidence, not five independent confirmations. Group evidence by source/mechanism.

## Asset Types expansion hooks

### `fx-seans-ve-makro-olay-riski`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/asset-schools-expansion/asset-fx-session-event-hook.*`
Teach: Asia/London/New York session timeline with liquidity/spread differences, overlaps, quiet periods, and a macro-event volatility window. “24/5” does not mean constant market quality.

### `emtiada-spot-ve-vadeli-fiyat`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/asset-schools-expansion/asset-commodity-spot-futures-hook.*`
Teach: spot plus several futures maturities as separate price references; storage, financing, delivery, and inventory can separate them.

### `contango-backwardation-ve-roll`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/asset-schools-expansion/asset-commodity-term-structure-hook.*`
Teach: upward/downward futures curves and an explicit roll from near to later contract. Spot return and futures-product return can differ.

### `kripto-perpetual-ve-funding`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/asset-schools-expansion/asset-crypto-perpetual-hook.*`
Teach: spot/index reference versus perpetual contract plus periodic funding payments between long/short sides. Funding may change sign and is not risk-free yield.

### `kripto-venue-custody-ve-karsi-taraf-riski`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/asset-schools-expansion/asset-crypto-custody-hook.*`
Teach: separate asset-price risk from exchange/venue, custody/key, collateral/stablecoin, transfer/network, and counterparty risks.

### `onchain-veri-ne-soyler-ne-soylemez`
Asset: `assets/learning/academy/asset-schools-expansion/asset-onchain-limits-hook.*`
Teach: observable transfer/balance activity versus uncertain owner identity, intent, off-chain trades, and internal venue accounting. Observation and inference must be visually separated.

## Integration rules

- SMC/ICT visuals use real price structures but must never imply manipulation, institutional intent, or guaranteed future direction as observed fact.
- Asset-type visuals emphasize product plumbing and exposure mechanics, not generic asset icons.
- Information-dense diagrams default to `contain`.
- Use exact batch filename + exact `slug + hook` registry mapping.
- Status remains `planned` until physical asset + registry mapping exist and both real-image and expansion batch audits pass.
